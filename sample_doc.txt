ACME TECHNOLOGIES — EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY (2026)

1. ANNUAL LEAVE
Every full-time employee is entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per
calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 days per completed month of service.
Unused annual leave up to a maximum of 10 days may be carried forward into
the next calendar year. Anything beyond 10 days lapses on 31 December.

2. SICK LEAVE
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate
is required for any absence longer than 2 consecutive working days. Sick
leave does not carry forward and is not encashable.

3. WORK FROM HOME
Employees may work from home up to 8 days per month with manager approval.
Requests must be raised on the HR portal at least 24 hours in advance.
Teams in client-facing roles are limited to 4 days per month.

4. NOTICE PERIOD
The notice period is 60 days for employees at Grade 3 and above, and 30 days
for Grade 1 and Grade 2. Notice period may be bought out at the discretion of
the reporting manager and HR.

5. PROBATION
New employees serve a probation period of 6 months. During probation, annual
leave accrues but cannot be availed except in emergencies approved by HR.
Confirmation is subject to a performance review at the end of month 5.

6. PUBLIC HOLIDAYS
The company observes 10 fixed public holidays and 2 optional holidays per
calendar year. The holiday calendar is published every December for the
following year.

7. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children and
12 weeks thereafter. Paternity leave is 15 days, to be availed within
6 months of the date of birth.

8. LEAVE APPROVAL
All leave requests must be approved by the reporting manager on the HR
portal. Leave taken without approval is treated as loss of pay. For annual
leave of more than 5 consecutive days, approval from the department head is
also required.
